# Road-Accident-Analysis-Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Road Accident Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze accident trends, casualty statistics, vehicle involvement, road conditions, and environmental factors affecting road accidents.

The dashboard transforms raw accident data into actionable insights that help government agencies, transportation authorities, and safety analysts identify accident patterns and improve road safety measures.

🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

Monitor accident casualties and severity levels.
Analyze accident trends over time.
Identify high-risk road and weather conditions.
Understand vehicle involvement in accidents.
Support data-driven road safety decisions.

🔍 Key Insights
🚨 Accident Severity
Slight accidents account for the largest proportion of casualties.
Fatal accidents represent a smaller percentage but require immediate attention.
🚗 Vehicle Insights
Cars contribute the highest number of accident casualties.
Motorcycles show relatively high risk compared to their volume.
🌧️ Environmental Factors
Wet road surfaces significantly increase accident occurrence.
Poor weather conditions contribute to higher accident risk.
🛣️ Road Insights
Single carriageways experience the highest number of accidents.
Urban areas record more accidents due to higher traffic density.
🌙 Visibility Insights
Reduced visibility conditions contribute to increased accident severity.
